    Game IQ concepts

    Decision making
    Spacing

    Stay on the proper side of the ball, create easy passing angles, and avoid crowding the lane when your teammate is driving downhill.

    Pick-and-roll decisions

    Read the defender’s hip, the screener’s roll timing, and whether the best action is a pass, a handoff, or a dribble attack.

    Defense

    Keep the ball in front, force the ball to the sideline when possible, and recover without chasing on a bad closeout.

    Quick strategy cues

    Memory prompt
    • When a teammate takes a defender deep, attack the opposite lane.
    • Never stand still on offense without a reason to move.
    • Use your eyes to make the right pass before the defender loads up.
    • Early help should be short and recoverable, not lazy.

    Play-style guide

    This is a practice-style suggestion, not a fixed position assessment. Your best role also depends on your team, coach, and skills.

    C

    Stephen Curry

    Guard · Shooting and off-ball movement

    Curry changes the geometry of the floor with shooting gravity, quick passes, and movement without the ball. His drills emphasize footwork, catch-and-shoot timing, and spacing discipline.

    Practice: Start close to the rim, make five balanced form shots, then move one step back after each set.

    Common mistake: drifting sideways or rushing the feet before the pass arrives.

    Level / time: Beginner to advanced · 12 minutes

    Measure: record makes out of 25 shots from five marked spots.

    K

    Kyrie Irving

    Point guard · Handles, creativity, and control

    Kyrie thrives in tight spaces and uses hesitation moves and body control to create openings. Work on dribble combinations, change of pace, and finishing through contact.

    Practice: Perform a controlled hesitation into a change of direction, then finish with either hand.

    Common mistake: over-dribbling or changing direction without protecting the ball.

    Level / time: Intermediate · 10 minutes

    Measure: count clean finishes from 10 attempts on each side.

    L

    Luka Dončić

    Guard · Vision, playmaking, and pace

    Luka sees the floor at a different speed, creating advantages with passing angles and tempo changes. His style requires patience, attention to weak-side defenders, and confident decision-making.

    Practice: Use a chair or cone as a screen; pause, read the imagined help defender, and choose a pass or change of pace.

    Common mistake: deciding before checking where the help defender is positioned.

    Level / time: Intermediate to advanced · 12 minutes

    Measure: track correct reads from 10 imagined pick-and-roll reps.

    S

    Shai Gilgeous-Alexander

    Guard · Efficiency, downhill attacks, and balance

    Shai blends smooth downhill speed with efficient finishing and controlled spacing. Drill your first step, second-step decisions, and one-dribble pull-ups off the catch or dribble.

    Practice: Attack from the wing at half speed, vary the gather, then finish balanced without forcing contact.

    Common mistake: taking extra steps or leaning past a balanced finish.

    Level / time: Beginner to advanced · 10 minutes

    Measure: compare controlled makes out of 10 from both sides.

    K

    Klay Thompson

    Shooting guard · Shooting and conditioning

    Study a shooting-guard approach built on balanced footwork, quick preparation, off-ball movement, and staying ready through a long possession.

    Practice: From five spots, move into a balanced catch-and-shoot stance and take five comfortable shots at each spot.

    Common mistake: rushing the release or letting the feet drift sideways as you get tired.

    Level / time: Beginner to advanced · 12 minutes

    Measure: record makes out of 25 and note whether each shot started balanced.

    T

    Jayson Tatum

    Forward · Two-way wing skills

    Build transferable wing habits with controlled shot preparation, strong closeouts, and reading whether to shoot, drive, or move the ball.

    Practice: Start on the wing, catch in balance, then choose a comfortable shot, one-dribble attack, or extra pass.

    Common mistake: deciding on the move before checking the defender and open space.

    Level / time: Intermediate · 12 minutes

    Measure: track good decisions out of 10 reps, not just made shots.

    G

    Giannis Antetokounmpo

    Forward · Rim pressure and transition

    Practice a forward’s ability to run the floor, attack open space under control, and use balanced gathers to finish without forcing contact.

    Practice: From the wing, take two controlled dribbles into open space and finish with a balanced gather; alternate sides.

    Common mistake: taking too many steps or charging into a crowded lane.

    Level / time: Beginner to advanced · 10 minutes

    Measure: count controlled finishes out of 10 on each side.

    J

    Nikola Jokić

    Center · Passing, screening, and touch

    Explore center skills through patient decisions, useful screens, soft touch near the basket, and passing to teammates when help defenders commit.

    Practice: Set a stationary screen for a partner, pivot to a passing target, and finish with a close-range touch shot.

    Common mistake: screening while moving or forcing a shot after the defense collapses.

    Level / time: Beginner to intermediate · 10 minutes

    Measure: log correct pass-or-finish choices across 10 repetitions.
